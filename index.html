The Ratel

This article is about my favorite animal

My favorite animal is called the honey badger also known as the ratel.

The honey badger is a powerfully built animal with short, strong legs and sharp claws, as well as equally sharp teeth.

They are jet-black in color except for a grey upper coat or mantle that sits on top of a white stripe that runs from the crown of its head to the base of the tail.

Interesting Facts about the Ratel

  • The ratel is a powerful and fearless animal.
  • They are known to attack animals much larger than themselves, including lions and buffalo.
  • They are also known to attack humans if they feel threatened.
